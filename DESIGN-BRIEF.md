# ניהול מלאי — build brief (read fully before writing code)

Hebrew, RTL, ten pages only: **סקירה** (`/`), **סגירת יום** (`/closing`), **מלאי** (`/inventory`), **3D** (`/3d`), **הזמנות** (`/orders`), **תנועות** (`/movements`), **תקלות** (`/faults`), **תיעוד** (`/documentation`), **ספקים** (`/suppliers`; until 2026-11-03 מיפוי קטלוג), **צוות** (`/team`). Do not add pages; new workflows go into tabs or side panels of these. **תיעוד** and **צוות** have no sidebar row: they open from the settings dialog (`SETTINGS_PAGES` in `AppShell.tsx`).
Stack: Next.js 16 App Router (`src/app`), React 19, TypeScript strict, Tailwind v4 (`src/app/globals.css`), Supabase JS.
All pages are **client components** driven by `useData()` from `src/lib/store.tsx` (realtime + optimistic writes) and `useUi()` from `src/components/app/ui-state.tsx`.
**No demo/mock data anywhere.** If a table is empty, render a quiet `EmptyState` that says what to do. Never hardcode names, numbers or rows.

## One site, three systems
This app is the one deployment: the manager's dashboard at `/` behind the login, the worker app at `/worker` and the agents' catalogue at `/catalog`, both open. `ShellGate` and `AuthGate` route between them, and `SystemSwitch` at the very bottom of every page moves between the three. The worker keeps `.wk-*` and `src/components/worker`, the catalogue keeps `.cg-*` under `.cg-root`, its own look (see `src/app/catalog/catalog.css`) and its own data in `src/lib/catalog`. A class name shared by two systems reaches both, so scope it.

## The design system (source of truth)
- `src/design/tokens.css` — CSS variables (colours, spacing, radii, heights, fonts). Use `var(--…)` or the Tailwind theme aliases in `globals.css` (e.g. `text-ink-2`, `bg-surface`, `border-line`).
- `src/design/components.css` — ready `sa-*` classes for every component. **Use these classes**; add Tailwind only for layout.
- `src/components/ui/` — the typed components, re-exported from `index.ts`.
- The admin's classic light theme sits one step deeper than the shared tokens (canvas, greys, rules). That override lives in `src/app/globals.css`.
- Font: in the default aluminium style (`minimal`, `minimal-dark`) the face is the platform's own through `system-ui`: San Francisco on a Mac or iPhone, Segoe on Windows, Roboto on Android, and no web font loads. Every digit in the product is tabular through `body`, so a figure never jumps and needs no class. `--font-mono` is the same family: `.sa-mono` only keeps a code left to right. No code font, anywhere; it is what made the product look like a terminal. The classic style keeps IBM Plex Sans Hebrew, self-hosted in `public/fonts/plex/`.
- `src/design/minimal.css` — the aluminium style's component overrides, scoped to `[data-theme^="minimal"]`, so light and dark share every shape and differ only in tokens. Tokens for it live in `tokens.css` after the `:root` block.
- `src/design/order.css` — order to the eye in the aluminium style. Shapes and type only, never a colour value. The root layout carries AppShell's CSS to every route, so its wrapper also asks for `:has(.sa-shell)` inside `:where()`: the login, the worker and the catalogue keep their own type, and the wrapper still outranks `[data-theme^="minimal"]`, so a rule there holds whichever stylesheet loads last.
- Small data pictures live in `src/components/app/Viz.tsx` (`StockMeter`, `SparkBars`, `CountUp`, `ProductThumb`), admin only.

## Design language (what "premium" means here)
The product is sold to factories for ₪150,000. It must read as a serious instrument, never as a template or a toy, and it must be a pleasure to look at for hours. Four styles, each in light and dark, chosen in the settings dialog (`src/lib/theme.tsx`: ids `light`, `dark`, `minimal`, `minimal-dark`, `minimal-bokeh`, `minimal-bokeh-dark`, `merhav`, `merhav-dark`). The aluminium style, id `minimal`, is the default; a saved choice stays:
- **Classic** (`light` / `dark`): the older language at the end of this section, graphite ink on warm paper.
- **Aluminium** (`minimal` / `minimal-dark`): designed the way Apple designs its own tools, for the people in an aluminium factory. On 2026-11-02 the owner said the ruled ledger looked like a system from the eighties, so the code font, the black rules, the filled table heads and the flat grey desk are gone for good. Do not bring them back.
  - **The audience:** a warehouse manager and a factory owner, not a designer. They read at arm's length, for hours, often on their feet. So: big type, near-black text, plain Hebrew, big targets, nothing that appears only on hover, and every state a word as well as a colour. A screen must answer "what needs me now" before anything else. Beauty never costs legibility: the owner has twice rejected pale and thin.
  - **Depth, not lines:** white sheets float on a cool silver desk (`#eeeef3`; dark: `#1c1c1e` on black). A card is a soft shadow (`--shadow-card`) with continuous corners and no border. Boxes are never drawn. A row rule is one light line that starts at the text, as on an iPhone. The sidebar and the drawer float clear of the window edge. Glass (`--bg-glass`) is for the toolbar only, where content really passes under it.
  - **Colour:** blue `#0071e3` is what you can press: the primary button, where you are in the sidebar, links, focus, a set filter. Green, orange and red say what the stock is doing, as a word, a figure or a bar of data, and they are vivid. Coloured text uses the `-text` tokens, which clear 4.5:1 on white. Goods in are green, goods out red on the figure. `--short` stays a step louder than `--danger-text`, and nothing else uses it.
  - **Data is the picture:** `StockMeter` is a battery whose middle mark is the minimum, `SparkBars` is the week beside a figure, and every product row leads with its own photo. A picture only where it shows real data. Everything works with empty data.
  - **Order to the eye (2026-11-03):** the owner's words: "סדר, איכות הקריאה, אני נכנס למסך אני יודע הכל". Blue, the silver desk and the white sheets stay exactly as they are; order means one type scale, one row, figures on shared edges and what needs a person first. A section stays on a white sheet, because coloured text clears 4.5:1 only there.
  - **Type:** the page title is 28/36 600, a card title 16/600, the name in a row 14/500 with a quiet 13/400 line under it, the figure a row ends on 16/600, a strip figure 28/36 600, and a count that leads a lane 44/52 600. One weight stops at 600 everywhere. Figures may tighten by -0.02em; Hebrew letter-spacing stays 0. Small text never goes below 13px, and `--text-quiet` is only for what is empty, unset or still to come.
  - **Controls:** tabs and choices are one segmented control whose thumb slides. A secondary button is a quiet fill, not an outlined box. A field is a white well that lights up blue, and a filter beside it is the same well. `--control-md` is 40, a table row 60 and its head 38, the sidebar 216 (196 under 1340), the top bar 64.
  - **The rail (2026-11-03):** the owner asked why there are so many pages and what each wants of them, so the eight rows stand in three chapters with a word over each (13/600, on the icons' edge; `NAV_GROUPS` in `AppShell.tsx`, this style only, hidden in a phone's row of tabs): מחכה לך (סקירה, תקלות, סגירת יום), המחסן (מלאי, 3D, תנועות), לקוחות וספקים (הזמנות, ספקים). Since 2026-11-04 each chapter folds under its word (`Sidebar.tsx`): the word is a button with a thin chevron at its end, down while open and pointing in while folded, and the rows glide shut under it in `--dur-2`; which chapters are folded stays with the browser (`sa-nav-folds`), and the chapter that holds the page you are on opens itself on arrival. A page whose title doesn't say what it is for says it in its sub line: 3D reads איפה כל דבר עומד, ספקים reads מי מביא מה, ואיך הוא קורא לזה.
  - **Sheets:** a drawer, a form and the settings dialog are a silver sheet holding white groups, like Settings on an iPhone: rules start at the text, one blue button sits at the foot (`src/components/app/drawers.css`, `.sa-sheet`, `.sa-group`, `.sa-rows`). The worker's phone screens are the same grouped lists on the same desk, with the largest type and targets in the product (`src/components/worker/worker.css`). The login is the one dark moment: `public/login/aluminium.webp`, a photograph of the stock on black, fills the window with the name on it in white, and the same silver sheet stands open over it where a drawer stands. Swap that one file for another factory's stock; the picture stays dark, because the name and the system links sit on it in white.
  - **Corners:** `corner-shape: squircle` where the browser has it, so radii are larger there (`--radius-md` 14, `--radius-lg` 26). Corners nest: an inner radius is the outer one minus the gap.
  - **Motion:** one curve (`--ease`) and three lengths (`--dur-1/2/3`: 120, 180, 220ms in the admin). A press answers at once (0.97, 0ms) and lets go softly. A page settles in one plain fade, nothing staggered. Bars grow and figures climb when data arrives, once, and never loop. Modals rise, menus drop, a drawer leaves the way it came. State is fill, never weight, so neighbours never move. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns it all off.

- **Bokeh** (`minimal-bokeh` / `minimal-bokeh-dark`, the third choice in the settings dialog): the control room. On 2026-11-02 the owner showed a dashboard from a large ERP and asked for that style with more colour than it has, on cards with depth and out-of-focus light behind them, in order and clean. The style is never named after that product.
  - **It is the aluminium in a new skin.** The ids start with `minimal`, so every `[data-theme^="minimal"]` rule still applies and every page keeps its shapes, sizes and motion; `src/design/bokeh.css` holds only the tokens and what a token can't do, and repeats what the aluminium keeps under an exact id (`--short`, the sheets, the 3D scene). A rule added under an exact id needs its twin there.
  - **Colour:** violet `#5b4fd6` is what you can press, on a lavender desk. The series are `--viz-1` to `--viz-5` (violet, teal, amber, rose, blue) and `--viz-other`, checked for colour blindness in both modes; a series keeps its hue everywhere and text never wears one.
  - **Depth:** `--shadow-card` is a lit rim, four pixels of the slab's own edge and the shadow it throws; `--shadow-lift` thickens the edge. Tiles lean toward a mouse by 5 degrees and a pressed one sinks into its edge. A finger and reduced motion get the same slab standing still.
  - **Bokeh:** one fixed layer of defocused discs behind the admin and the login, never moving, and two lights of a tile's own hue behind its glass. This is the one place the "no gradients, no glow" rule gives way, because the owner asked for it. It stays out of the worker's phone and the catalogue.
  - **The overview becomes the control room** (`src/components/app/dashboard`, sums in `src/lib/control-room.ts`): six tiles, then a grid of charts, in five views (מנהל, מכירות, מלאי, רכש, תפעול) and three periods, both in the URL. Tiles always read 30 days against the 30 before, so they mean the same on the 2nd of a month as on the 28th. Charts are hand drawn in `Charts.tsx`: one axis, thin marks, a legend for two series or more, time left to right, a table twin one button away. "No chart of the past" holds for the other two styles.

- **Merhav** (`merhav` / `merhav-dark`, label מרחב, the fourth choice in the settings dialog, admin only): the warehouse is the interface. Nothing from the other styles applies here: the id does not start with `minimal`, so it stands on the bare `sa-*` classes and `src/design/merhav/*` re-skins them. `src/lib/theme.tsx` paints `/worker` and `/catalog` as `minimal` while it is chosen, so those two systems never see it, and the worker's settings dialog doesn't offer it.
  - **The one idea:** one live model of the warehouse stands under the whole admin, in `src/components/merhav/twin/Twin.tsx`, on the unchanged engine of `src/lib/map3d`. It is built once and survives every page turn. It stands directly on the page's own surface, with no frame, so the floor of the UI is the floor of the model. Every list is a lens on it: hover a row and that place lights up and a thin line runs to it from the door's edge (`aimProduct`, `Leader.tsx`); open a product and the camera goes to its real shelf beside the panel (`PanelFocus.tsx`).
  - **The one layout:** a world (fixed, the model), a door (`.mh-door`, one tall plate with one long straight edge and the shadow it throws on the model), and a rail that houses the door. The door stands in four places: `open` (tucked into the rail, סקירה), `dock` (the model keeps about a third, the lists), `side` (a narrow door beside a full model, מחסן), `closed` (it covers the model, pages with no use for it). `DOOR` in `Shell.tsx` decides; the model re-centres in what is left through `setInsets`. No cards. A section is a rule and a title on the plate. What truly rises (a menu, a dialog, a drawer, the search) is a plate with a shadow.
  - **The rail** is a scale cut into the housing: words against a ruled edge, one green carriage that rides it to where you are. No icons in it. The search is invisible until wanted: start typing anywhere, or ⌘K. Walking through the results lights each product on the model.
  - **Material:** matte anodised white in warm neutrals (`--bg-surface` #f7f6f2 on a world of #e6e4dd), deep graphite ink (#131614), one deep green (`--accent` #0d4f3c) for what you can press and what is alive. Rules are ink at a strength, so they sit right on every surface in both modes. Light falls from the rail's side, so every shadow runs the same way. Radii are machined: 3, 5, 8, 12.
  - **Type:** IBM Plex Sans Hebrew in its light cuts, self-hosted under its own family name `mh-plex` (`src/design/merhav/fonts.css`): a face named like the other styles' Plex would replace theirs. Numbers are the heroes: large and weight 200 to 300, tight, tabular, and they turn like a machined counter (`Figure.tsx`, one wheel per digit, only for figures that lead a screen; tables use plain text). Words are small and few. Text never goes below 13px.
  - **Motion:** real springs sampled into `linear()` (`--spring-press`, `--spring-move`, `--spring-plate`, `--spring-settle`). A press answers at once. The door moves on `--spring-plate`. The carriage rides on `--spring-move`. Nothing loops, nothing waits for an animation, waiting is one thin line (`.sa-skeleton`). Reduced motion turns it all off.
  - **Colour on the number:** goods in `--qty-in`, goods out `--danger-text`, low `--warning-text`, and `--short` the one loud mark, on the figure and the word חסר only. Every state is a word as well as a colour.
  - **סקירה:** the model takes the stage, and the figures interlock with the empty corners of its L: worth, what is free and held, cover, then the worries, the week and today's movements as ticks along a 24 hour line. Scrolling lifts a sheet over the model with everything else. Since 2026-11-03 the sheet opens with three lanes, תקלות, אישורים and חסר להזמנות, each a count and its first three rows with "עוד N" unfolding in place, then מה ייגמר ראשון beside הזמנות פתוחות; nothing on the sheet repeats a list another page owns.
  - **מלאי:** the skyline is every product as one bar, worst first, on a log scale with the minimum cut across it, and it is also the filter: the figures under it are the tabs. Same `?filter=`.
  - **The door by hand:** on a dock page the door's edge can be dragged, which re-centres the model and is remembered per browser (`DoorHandle.tsx`). Opening a product there unfolds the drawer from the door's edge and the camera goes to the real shelf beside it. On סקירה the drawer is the door itself, coming out of the rail. What stands on the door shows only where the door has reached, and סקירה's figures stay on the world while the door closes over them (`Shell.tsx`, `Overview.tsx`), so nothing is ever there before its surface. A figure never counts up on arrival: its wheels turn only when the value changes.

The ledger and cards are the shared map3d components restyled; every row reads its own shelf and reaches for it with the line. A walk opens the door all the way and you step into the model. Since 2026-11-03 the page has no tabs: one list by place (sorted by product, place, stock or share of the minimum) and the walk; orders, movements and worth have their own pages, where the model stands beside them. Rules that hold in every style:
- **No tiles.** No letter marks ("מ" in a square), no coloured squares behind icons, no initials in discs, no status pills, no chips with fills. The product name is set in type, with nothing beside it.
- **Calm.** Urgency comes from position, not volume. No red borders, warning triangles, solid red pills or red notification discs. The one exception is a form field with a validation error: red border and red message, because the worker must find it.
- **Icons are thin, bare and grey, and help the eye find a word; they never replace it.** They sit beside navigation, the actions menu and its items, stat labels, movement types, attention groups, search results and buttons that act. No icons on card titles or empty states, and no tile behind them.
- **No sparkle.** No glow, shimmer, gradients on surfaces (the bokeh style's lights excepted), AI-star icons or flashing. No uppercase, no tracked-out labels, no dashed borders. The worker document scan stays as it is.
- **A sign appears only when it says something.** The top bar's line (`LiveStatus.tsx`) states facts: when the store last heard the database and how many workers moved stock today; offline it says מתחבר. The worker has no login, so nothing may claim who is connected. No keyboard-hint chips. No dead buttons, except יום הבא on today's closing, which holds its place so יום קודם never moves under the finger.
- **Copy is short.** Title, at most one short line. Buttons name what they do. No "דחוף" drama. No sentences that explain the obvious.

The classic style keeps its older limits: graphite primary button, 1px rules, radii of 3 to 6px, 0.1s linear motion, page title 24/32, weight 600 at most.

Layout: content max-width 1340px, padding `--space-8` / `--space-10` (24px inline and a 208px sidebar from 900 to 1339, so lists keep their room). Overview, since 2026-11-03: three chapters, each a word on the desk (20/26 600) with what the owner does there beside it in one short line (`Section` in `src/components/app/Snapshot.tsx`, sums in `src/lib/snapshot.ts`), because the owner asked what each section wants of them. **מחכה לך** · כל שורה נפתחת בלחיצה, ומה שטופל יורד מכאן (הכל תקין · אין מה לטפל עכשיו when nothing waits): one sheet of three lanes in reading order, תקלות, אישורים, חוסרים, each a large count, a verb line under it (לטפל, ולסגור עם תיעוד · לאשר או לדחות · להזמין מהספק) and its first three rows; every row opens what it names, and the X that removes a row lives only in the stock lane, its width kept so the words share one edge. **המספרים** · רק לדעת, כל מספר פותח את הפירוט שלו: one strip of four figures (שווי מלאי, הזמנות פתוחות, יצא השבוע, נכנס השבוע), rules between them; since 2026-11-04 the two week figures carry the change against the week before as a signed percent on the note (green up, red down, the week before in brackets) and נכנס השבוע is green on the figure itself. Under the strip one sheet of three rings (`PieSheet` in `Snapshot.tsx`, `Ring` in `Viz.tsx`, sums in `snapshot.ts`): שווי המלאי by category, מכירות by customer from the orders delivered in the last 30 days, נכנס מספקים by supplier over the same days, every ₪ במחירון; the five biggest parts in a fixed palette order and אחרים in grey, the total in the middle, a legend with the ₪ quiet and the share in ink; pointing at a part or its row thickens it and reads its share in the middle, each head opens its page, and the ring draws itself once on arrival. No chart of the past over time: a figure or a word says it; the rings show a whole and its parts, which the owner asked to see on 2026-11-04.

A fault's drawer holds the one field that closes it, and that note goes through the same guard as a form. Since 2026-11-03 a form shows only what is a must and the rest waits under one collapsed עוד פרטים (`src/components/app/forms/MoreFields.tsx`): a product is שם, מק"ט (empty gets the next `P-0001` code), כמות and יחידה, מינימום, מחיר, ספק ראשי, קטגוריה, תמונה; goods in is ספק, מוצר, כמות, מיקום; goods out is מוצר, כמות, לקוח, קבלה. The product details drop what the history already tells (נוצר, עודכן, תנועות החודש) and the unit and barcode.

The top search is also the way to act (`src/components/app/command-search.ts`, ⌘K, also on a Hebrew keyboard). Empty, it offers what has something waiting plus קליטה and הוצאה. Typed, it answers questions with a count ("איחור" gives הזמנות באיחור · 2), then products and orders, then customers.

An empty state says what happened and, when there is a next step, carries the button that takes it.

Page anatomy, one for all nine: every page is `.sa-page`. `PageHead` has one height everywhere: the title on the button line, one quiet fact no tab states (its line stays reserved, so nothing below moves), actions at the end with at most one primary. Then `Toolbar` or the first surface, then surfaces `--space-5` apart. `Toolbar` puts tabs at the start, filters beside them and the search at the far end at 320, on every page, on one line until it can't hold them. Creating is a plus icon. A footer appears only when something is hidden (`Showing`).

A filter row with several filters waits for החל (`/movements`): tabs and search answer at once, while the menus, the calendar and the hours only choose. החל applies them and reads the database again, so it works with nothing new chosen, and the press shows one small refresh: a thin line across the top of the list while the rows step back, then ✓ עודכן on the button for a moment. Days and hours are picked in `src/components/app/WhenFilters.tsx`, one floating sheet where a range is drawn by pointing at its two ends. No native date or time field in a filter row.

The **3D** page is the warehouse at a glance, and the one page with no column limit: the rail at the inline start holds the page head, three summary figures, the tabs and a ruled ledger of every product (place, quantity, percent of its minimum, the shared `StockMeter`), what needs a hand first and every column named. The stage takes the rest to the bottom edge on `--scene-canvas`. The scene is soft and serious: matte sage and neutral forms in real proportions, no ink lines, no wire, nothing toylike. A rule on the floor marks what needs a hand, and the tags use the same percent as the ledger. `products.qty` is the only quantity: every piece on a shelf is drawn, so the shelf can be counted against the ledger (stock sold by the metre stands in whole bars, and a rack that can't hold its count at the large drawn size draws all it holds smaller), every number is DOM from the store, never a canvas texture, and quantities never sum across products. Every ₪ is a sale price and says במחירון. Machines and zones are geometry with no status. The forklift stands where goods were last loaded, the latest in or out movement with a place. Motion comes only from a new `movements.id`. Any close view has one named exit button on the stage. A rack's close view comes from its front corner, a little above the shelves, and holds that rack alone on the floor: no neighbours and no forklift. In the light style colour tells things apart: warm floor, blue frames, orange beams, stock in its own tones. The past view is read only and hides status and ₪. `--scene-*` tokens live in `src/components/map3d/map3d.css`, never in `src/design`, and `three` is reached only through `import()` inside an effect.

A rack's card is a ledger of its shelves, top shelf first: place, product, how much stands here, its percent of the minimum. A product on neighbouring places is one block: two halves draw as one pile, shelves one above the other share a line and a tag. The percent is the product's own, the same figure as the rail, so a shelf never tells a different story. Tags stand on the shelf: from afar only what needs a hand, as one figure; close up every shelf, with the product, its quantity and the empty places. A piece that can't fit half a shelf (a 6 m bar) isn't offered one, and two of them share a shelf across its depth.

## Data model (Supabase, `src/lib/database.types.ts`)
products(id, sku, barcode, name, category, unit, qty, reserved, min_qty, location, primary_supplier_id, notes, in_catalog, description, price, image_path, created_at, updated_at) — qty is what is physically on the shelf; reserved is what open orders hold, kept only by the order functions, and no client ever writes it. Available is qty − reserved (`available(p)` in the store), negative when the shelf fell below what is held. Photos live in the public bucket `product-photos`, managed by the signed-in admin (`src/lib/product-photos.ts`).
movements(id, type in|out|fault|adjust, product_id, qty, worker_id, supplier_id, reference, note, place, created_at) — a DB trigger keeps products.qty in sync; the store does the same optimistically. place says where a receipt was put away.
shelf_stock(id, product_id, place, qty, placed_at, updated_at) — how much of products.qty stands where; a place is `A-1/2`, its halves `A-1/2R` and `A-1/2L` as seen from the aisle, or `F-01`. Read by anyone, written only by the `movements_shelves` trigger and `place_stock`. A receipt with a place adds to that row; anything that lowers the total takes what waits for a place first, then the oldest shelf. What no row covers shows under ללא מיקום. products.location stays the product's home, used for a suggestion and for a product at zero. `usual_places` counts where each product was received, for the one-tap suggestion.
faults(id, product_id, movement_id, description, reported_by, status open|resolved, created_at, resolved_at, resolution, resolved_by) — a fault closes only with a written resolution; the database stamps who closed it (`faults_stamp_resolution`), and `20261103_fault_resolution_required.sql` makes the note a rule.
suppliers(id, name, contact, phone, email, notes)
supplier_items(id, supplier_id, external_name, external_sku, product_id, status approved|pending)
workers(id, name, role, active)
agents(id, name, phone, active, created_at) — insert and update for the signed-in admin only; never deleted, an agent is deactivated like a worker.
orders(id, number, request_id, agent_id, customer, customer_phone, note, status open|delivered|cancelled, created_at, closed_at) — number is the human order number, from 1001.
order_lines(id, order_id, product_id, qty, unit_price, movement_id) — unit_price is the price when ordered and may be null; movement_id is set on delivery.

**Orders are written only through RPCs.** `orders` and `order_lines` are read-only to clients. The catalogue site places an order with `create_order`, which holds its stock. The admin closes one with `deliver_order` (one out movement per line, the order closed, the hold released) or `cancel_order` (the order closed, the hold released, the stock never moved). Each is atomic and raises a Hebrew message; show it as it is.

**A closed day doesn't change.** `day_summary(date)` works out one Israeli calendar day as JSON, including what stood open when it ended, for the signed-in admin only. The `day-closing` job (pg_cron, hourly) stores every ended day in `day_closings`, which no client can write. `/closing` reads a closed day from that table and today from the function, so both show the same thing.

**Closing is a process.** The first card says where the day stands in one sentence, with its one action; the figures follow the day (נקלט, נמסר, פתוח, מעכב); then what stands in the way, what passes to tomorrow, what came in beside what left, open orders and manager actions. Faults, unrecognised lines and supplier names hold a day back; stock under its minimum follows it out without holding it. `sign_day(target, why)` puts a manager's name on today or yesterday in `day_signoffs`, read-only to clients, and refuses without a written `why` while anything is open. The signature sits beside the frozen record and never changes it; midnight still closes the day. `exit_pass_story(pass)` says who approved an exit with no receipt, for a movement's drawer.

`useData()` returns: `state.{products,movements,suppliers,supplierItems,faults,workers,agents,orders,orderLines,status,error,live}`, lookups `product(id)`, `supplier(id)`, `worker(id)`, `agent(id)`, `order(id)`, `linesOf(orderId)`, derived `attention` (sorted AttentionEntry[]), `stats`, `weekly` (7 DayPoint), and writes: receive, issue, adjust, reportFault, resolveFault, reopenFault, createProduct, updateProduct, deleteProduct, createSupplier, updateSupplier, deleteSupplier, createWorker, updateWorker, createAgent, updateAgent, deliverOrder, cancelOrder, createSupplierItem, mapSupplierItem, approveSupplierItem, unapproveSupplierItem, deleteSupplierItem, fetchProductHistory. All writes return promises and throw `Error(message)` on failure — show the error in a `Notice tone="danger"` next to the form, and `notify({tone:"success", …})` on success. The store also exports `available(p)` and `orderTotal(lines)`.

`useUi()` returns: `panel`, `openAction(kind, ctx)`, `openProduct(id, tab?)`, `openOrder(id)`, `openFault(id)`, `openMovement(row)`, `close()`, `requestClose()`, `toasts`, `notify`, `dismiss`. Hosts: `src/components/app/ActionDrawer.tsx` (all action forms), `ProductPanel.tsx`, `OrderPanel.tsx`, `FaultPanel.tsx` and `MovementPanel.tsx` in the same folder are rendered by `AppShell`, in every style.

Formatting helpers in `src/lib/format.ts`: fmtQty, fmtPrice, fmtCompact, fmtUnit, fmtSigned, fmtTime, fmtDate, fmtDateTime, fmtRelative, fmtWhen, MOVEMENT_LABEL, ORDER_STATUS_LABEL. A unit beside a number goes through fmtUnit: 3 אריזות, 1 אריזה.

## Copy rules
Hebrew only in UI; codes/SKUs Latin. No exclamation marks. One concept, one word, in both apps:
- **Processes:** קליטת סחורה (קליטה), הוצאת סחורה (הוצאה), דיווח תקלה, תיקון ספירה. The worker's own first person stays on its home rows and questions: קיבלתי סחורה, מסרתי סחורה ללקוח, למי מסרת.
- **Objects:** שורה is one line of a document. A document's number is neither asked for nor shown: the photo is the record, and what a record shows of its reference is only the order it came from, under מקור. מוצר חדש for an item the warehouse doesn't have. מק"ט, ספק, התאמה, חריגה stay. The archive tabs קבלות הכנסה and קבלות הוצאה are the owner's words.
- **The manager:** the worker reads אצל המנהל on a line, העברה למנהל on the button, יועבר למנהל before saving, הועבר למנהל after. The admin reads ממתין.
- **Orders:** הזמנה is what a סוכן places for a לקוח. משוריין is stock an open order holds. זמין is what can still be promised: on the shelf minus משוריין. When משוריין passes the shelf, the gap reads חסר and the product is חסר להזמנות. An order is פתוחה until it closes as נמסרה (the goods left, the button is סימון כנמסרה) or בוטלה (the button is ביטול הזמנה).
- **Faults:** a fault is פתוחה until it closes as טופלה, through סגירה עם תיעוד and a note under מה נעשה. There is no one-press סימון כטופל.
- **Goods leaving:** an exit needs its קבלה, photographed. With none, only a מנהל ראשי goes on: בלי קבלה, a סיבה and a code to the phone. That role never reads סופר אדמין on a screen.
- **Buttons are action nouns, gender neutral:** שמירה, אישור, ביטול, מחיקה, הסרה, ניסיון נוסף. The one exception is החל on filters, the owner's own word. A button that opens a form names the thing with a plus (ספק חדש); the button that commits adds it (הוספת ספק).
- **Sentences never address a man:** אפשר להסיר את הסינון. Empty: אין {noun}. No match: לא נמצאו {noun}. Placeholders: חיפוש לפי..., no ellipsis.
